# Freelance Marketplace Facts (re-verify before use — these drift)

## Contra
- Payout via Stripe; 0% commission (the client pays $29 per one-time contract, or $29/mo for ongoing).
- Minimum: **$250 flat / $25 per hour**. Sub-$250 tiers cannot be listed.
- "Identity Verified" badge is auto-granted when a bank account is linked via Stripe (no separate application).
- Positioning (observed 2026-10): creative-first — brand design, UGC, motion, video dominate the homepage; "Web developers" / "AI developers" roles exist but are minor. Weak demand side for code/dev services.

## Upwork
- Payout: Direct-to-Local-Bank **$0.99/withdrawal** (JPY supported) is the cheapest; Payoneer $2; wire $50.
- Tax: W-8BEN + Statement of Source required; missing forms → up to 30% withholding + blocked withdrawals.
- Poor fit for premium async offers: clients often expect an intro call and compare against low hourly rates.

## Fiverr
- Payout: bank transfer via Payoneer ($1, min $20 outside US), PayPal, Payoneer account ($3). Direct deposit is US-only.
- Race-to-bottom pricing culture; use only for a lead-magnet tier (e.g. $199), not premium tiers.

## W-8BEN (Japan seller)
- Individual: Form W-8BEN. Entity: Form W-8BEN-E. TIN = My Number (マイナンバー).
- Japan-US tax treaty allows reduced/exempt withholding — claim it; do not skip the treaty section.
