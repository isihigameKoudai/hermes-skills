# Tokenomics scoring checklist (run before trusting a narrative)

- **Max supply** — fixed vs uncapped. Uncapped = permanent inflation risk (e.g. IOTA Rebased).
- **Circulating %** — remaining dilution runway. High founder/treasury lockup = future sell pressure (e.g. XDC ~40% circulating, 40% founder allocation).
- **Emission/inflation** — daily mint, % annual; is it offset by burn?
- **Burn** — fee-burn %, does it scale with usage, or is it a rounding error vs mint (compare mint/day vs burn/quarter)?
- **Founder/team allocation %** — above ~20–30% is a centralization red flag.
- **Consensus centralization** — masternode count, council governance, and can supply be changed (unanimous consent vs discretionary)?
- **Staking reward funding** — fees vs foundation subsidy. A finite subsidy pool is a security cliff when it drains (e.g. Algorand: rewards ~9.1 ALGO/block subsidy vs ~0.05 ALGO/block fees).
- **Red flags** — supply cap raised or removed retroactively; discretionary "central-bank-window" monetary policy; rewards drawn from a finite pool with no fee growth to replace it.
